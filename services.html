What We Offer

One crew for every stage of your home — from a fresh coat of paint to the whole house.

Estimates are free, quotes are written and itemized, and our advice is honest — including when the honest answer is that a project isn’t worth doing yet. Here’s what we build, and how we build it.

Start the Conversation

Inside & Out

Interior & Exterior Painting

Most paint jobs fail before the lid ever comes off — in the prep nobody wanted to do. We scrape, sand, patch, prime, and caulk until the surface deserves the finish. Then we cut clean lines, roll even coats, and leave you with colors that still look right years from now.

  • Whole-home interiors, trim, doors, and cabinets
  • Full exterior repaints with weather-grade finishes
  • Color consultation before the first gallon is bought
  • Drop cloths everywhere, and a site swept every night
Outdoor Structures

Fences & Decks

A fence should run straight and a gate should swing true — for years, not seasons. We build cedar privacy fences, gates, decks, pergolas, and railing that are plumb, level, square, and to code, with posts set deep and hardware made for Alabama weather.

  • Cedar privacy fences and custom gates
  • Decks, pergolas, and railing built to code
  • Every post plumb, every line checked twice
  • A written, itemized quote before we dig a single hole
Kitchens, Baths & Basements

Remodeling & Renovation

A remodel shouldn’t feel like moving out of your own house. We start with careful planning and an honest scope, handle the permits, put up real dust barriers, and communicate every day. We define what “finished” means before demo day — and then we hit it.

  • Kitchens, bathrooms, basements, and whole-room refreshes
  • Planning and permits handled for you
  • Dust control, nightly cleanup, daily updates
  • A defined finish line, not an open-ended project
The Small Stuff

Repairs & Punch Lists

Every house has the list: the sticking door, the soft deck board, the drywall crack, the gate that drags. Most contractors won’t return the call for jobs that small. We treat your punch list like an audition — handled carefully, priced fairly, and finished the first time.

  • One visit, a whole list handled
  • Carpentry, drywall, trim, doors, and rot repair
  • Itemized pricing — you approve every line
  • The same care we bring to a full remodel
For Businesses

Commercial & General Contracting

Storefronts, offices, and tenant spaces — refreshed without shutting you down. We plan the work around your business hours, take the noisy phases to nights and weekends when it helps, and leave the space clean enough to open every morning.

  • Tenant improvements and light build-outs
  • Storefront refreshes, paint, signage prep, and repairs
  • Scheduling built around your operating hours
  • Licensed, insured, and tidy — every day of the job
Where We’re Headed

Custom Home Construction

We’ll say it plainly: ground-up custom homes are where CB Construction is headed — deliberately, and not before we’ve earned it. Every fence line is practice for a foundation. Every remodel is a rehearsal for a whole house. The discipline you see on our small jobs — written scopes, tidy sites, daily communication — is the discipline we’re carrying to the big one.

If you own land, or you’re a year or three from breaking ground, talk to us early. We’d rather grow into your project alongside you than meet you the week you need plans.

Our Four-Step
Process
01

Reach Out

Call, email, or send the form. We’ll schedule a same-week walkthrough and take a good look at the job. The estimate is free — and so is a straight answer if we’re not the right fit.

02

Scope & Quote

You get a written, itemized quote with a fixed price. Every line is explained: what we’re doing, what it costs, and what would change it. No allowances hiding in the fine print.

03

Build

We show up when we said we would, protect everything we’re not working on, sweep the site every night, and send you an update every day. No surprises is the whole point.

04

Walkthrough & Stand-Behind-It

We walk the finished work together, write the punch list, and finish it. And if something isn’t right after we leave, call — we come back and make it right.
