The Job, In Order

How a Birmingham-area kitchen job actually runs

01

Walk the kitchen and the house around it

Birmingham-area kitchens sit in older closed-off rooms, ranch plans, and newer open layouts. We walk how you cook and gather, and we note which city the house sits in so the estimate is honest about process.

02

Scope in writing before demo

You get a written scope before we open a wall, so the household can plan around the work. What we are doing, what it costs, and what would change it.

03

Cabinets, counters, layout, lighting

Keep the existing openings when they still work. Change the layout when the traffic path is the real problem. Counters, lighting, and plumbing moves follow the same written plan.

04

Protect finished rooms

Floors, adjacent trim, and rooms you are still living in get protected. We clean as we go. Occupied houses do not get treated like empty shells.

05

Punch list before we leave

We walk the finished kitchen together, write the punch list, and finish it before we call the job done.

Scope, Not Guesswork

What a Birmingham-area kitchen remodel usually includes

Every house is different. Typical kitchen remodel work can include:

  • Cabinets and hardware
  • Counters and backsplash
  • Lighting in the kitchen zone
  • Flooring where the kitchen meets adjacent rooms
  • Plumbing and electrical as the plan requires

We help you understand what your project needs before work starts, including permits when the scope requires them. Permit path depends on the city and the work.
