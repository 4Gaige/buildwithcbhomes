The Job, In Order

How a Birmingham-area bath job actually runs

01

Walk the wet areas and leak history

Which bath, how you use it, soft floors, ventilation, and what has already been patched. We note which city the house sits in so the estimate is honest about process.

02

Diagnose the substrate

When the job needs it, we look at what is behind the tile: pan, board, plumbing, and whether a cosmetic refresh would fail. Waterproofing honesty starts here, not after demo.

03

Scope in writing before demo

You get a written scope before we open a wet wall. What we are doing, what it costs, and what would change it. Shower-only versus a full bath is part of that conversation when the wet area needs a larger fix.

04

Protect adjacent rooms

Occupied houses do not get treated like empty shells. Adjacent rooms get protected. We clean as we go. Sequence the wet work so one trade does not wreck the last.

05

Punch list before we leave

We walk the finished bath together, write the punch list, and finish it before we call the job done.

Scope, Not Guesswork

What a Birmingham-area bath remodel usually includes

Every house is different. Typical bathroom remodel work can include:

  • Showers and tubs
  • Tile and waterproofing assemblies
  • Vanities, flooring, and lighting
  • Ventilation as the wet area needs it
  • Plumbing moves the plan requires

A shower-only remodel is possible when the rest of the wet area is sound. We will say when a larger fix is the honest path. We help you understand what your project needs before work starts, including permits when the scope requires them. Permit path depends on the city and the work.
