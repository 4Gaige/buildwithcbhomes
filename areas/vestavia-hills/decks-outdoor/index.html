On a Vestavia Jobsite

How a Vestavia deck job actually runs

01

Walk the yard and how you gather

Shade, privacy, access, grade, and how the house attaches to the outdoor space. Vestavia yards often sit against trees, slopes, and neighboring sightlines. We walk how you actually use them before anyone talks products.

02

Structure and attachment honesty

Posts, framing, fastening, and waterproofing at the ledger where the job needs it. We say what the structure can carry, not only what the render wants.

03

Scope in writing

You get a written scope before posts go in the ground. What we are doing, what it costs, and what weather or access would change.

04

Protect the house and landscape

We protect the house and the yard as practical. Sequence framing, finish, and weather windows so they do not fight each other.

05

Punch list before we leave

We walk the finished work together, write the punch list, and finish it before we call the job done.

Structure First

Structure first, then the living layer

Posts, framing, fastening, and finish matched to use. A covered porch, a rebuild, or a new deck all start with structure that will take Alabama weather. The living layer (rails, ceilings, fireplace, seating) sits on that, not instead of it.

Typical scope pieces, always project-specific:

  • New decks and rebuilds
  • Rails, stairs, and covered structures
  • Outdoor living zones and related framing
  • Finish that matches how the space is used
