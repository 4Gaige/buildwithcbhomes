What’s Included

What a bathroom remodel usually includes

A cosmetic refresh is new paint and fixtures over a room that still works. A real remodel often includes waterproofing, substrate, plumbing, and ventilation decisions. We say which one you actually have before work starts.

  • Showers and tubs
  • Tile and waterproofing assemblies
  • Vanities, flooring, and lighting
  • Ventilation as the wet area needs it
  • Plumbing moves the plan requires

We do not publish dollar ranges or average timelines. A free estimate is the honest number.

We run the whole bath job as the GC. Tile, showers, waterproofing, and the trades the plan needs, under one written scope. That is the work. Not a pretty finish photo with a rushed pan behind it.
