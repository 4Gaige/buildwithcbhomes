Questions

Exterior painting FAQ

How does the estimate work?

Call or send the form below. We schedule a walkthrough, look at elevations and surface condition, and send a written next step. Scope comes in writing before prep starts.

What about weather delays?

Exterior paint depends on dry surfaces and workable temperatures. Rain and extreme heat can pause a day. We plan around the forecast and tell you when a pause is the right call.

Do you paint brick, wood siding, and trim?

Wood siding, trim, doors, and many painted surfaces are common. Brick and masonry finishes depend on condition and product. We will say what belongs in scope on the visit.

Are you only painters, or a remodel GC?

We are a remodel GC. Painting, kitchens, baths, decks, flooring, and related single-job work are the core. Custom homes are a deliberate path we are growing toward. This page is about exterior painting.
