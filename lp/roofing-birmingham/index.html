What’s Included

Repair, replacement, and the details that keep water out

Typical roofing scope pieces, always project-specific:

  • Roof inspection and leak-path conversation
  • Repair of damaged shingles, flashing, or penetrations when that is the right fix
  • Full or partial replacement when repair is not enough
  • Underlayment and ventilation decisions written into scope
  • Cleanup of tear-off debris when replacement is in scope
  • Siding or related envelope work only when written into the same plan

We do not publish dollar ranges or average timelines. A free estimate after we see the roof is the honest number, and it is also how capacity gets confirmed.

Find the water path, choose repair or replace in writing, and schedule only what we can finish well. That is the work.

Questions

Roofing FAQ

How does the estimate work?

Call or send the form below. Share what you are seeing (leak, missing shingles, age) and photos if you have them. We schedule a look when we can, write repair versus replace options, and confirm whether we have capacity before anyone starts tear-off.

Do you always recommend a full replacement?

No. Some roofs need a targeted repair. Some need a full or partial replacement. We will say which path fits what we see, in writing, instead of defaulting to the largest bid.

What if you are booked?

Capacity is real. If we cannot take the job on a timeline that helps you, we will tell you on the estimate path rather than string you along.

Are you a roofing-only company?

No. We are a remodel GC. Roofing and siding sit with our exterior work alongside painting, decks, kitchens, baths, and related single-job work. This page is about roofing.
