The backyard should work as hard as the kitchen

Tired deck boards. Unsafe rails. No shade. Outdoor space that never became a real room. Fences and grades that fight how the family actually uses the yard. Those are the problems a deck or outdoor living project is supposed to solve.

Alabama sun, rain, and humidity punish shortcuts on structure and finish. The fear is a pretty deck that softens early, or a half-finished outdoor kitchen with no weather plan.

CB Built Homes is a Birmingham-area remodel GC. Decks and outdoor living are in scope today. Custom homes are a deliberate later path, not the hero claim on this page.

Deck Staining

Already have a wood deck? We clean, stain, and seal it.

Gray, weathered boards do not always mean you need a new deck. For existing wood decks, we clean the surface, apply stain, and seal it so the wood looks cared for again.

Stain-only jobs are welcome. If we find rotten or soft boards on the visit, we will point them out. Those may need repair or replacement before stain goes on.
