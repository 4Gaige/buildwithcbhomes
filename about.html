About CB
A growing contractor in the greater Birmingham area

CB Construction is a Birmingham-area contractor built on hands-on trade experience — painting, carpentry, remodeling — and run on a simple conviction: the way a crew handles a small job tells you everything about how they’d handle a big one.

We also know why hiring a contractor feels like a gamble. Homeowners tell us the same worries again and again — surprise costs, quality shortcuts, phones that stop ringing, schedules that slip. So we built the company around their opposites: free written estimates itemized line by line, prep work we never skip, a call back every time, and realistic dates we honor. If something changes, you know the same day we do.

here’s the thing you need to know —
we don’t do “good enough for a small job.”

Home projects are among the largest investments a family will ever make, and we treat them that way. A fence, a fresh coat of paint, a whole kitchen — each one gets the same written scope, the same careful prep, and the same walk-through at the end, because each one is the house we’ll be judged by.
