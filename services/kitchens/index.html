A kitchen remodel should fix how the room works, not just how it photographs

Cramped layouts. Cabinets that no longer close true. Lighting that leaves the counters in shadow. Storage that never caught up with the household. Finishes that look tired next to the rest of the house. Those are the problems a kitchen remodel is supposed to solve.

Greater Birmingham homeowners often want one contractor who can run the whole job, not a parts list from five vendors and a hope that someone coordinates. The fear is living in dust for months with no written plan, or a crew that vanishes after demo.

The Job, In Order

How we approach a kitchen

01

Walk the space

We walk the kitchen and how you actually cook and gather. Traffic paths, storage that fails, light that is missing, and the rooms the kitchen opens into all go on the list before anyone talks products.

02

Scope in writing

You get a written scope before demo. What we are doing, what it costs, and what would change it. No parts list handed off to five vendors with a hope that someone coordinates.

03

Build the plan

Cabinets, counters, layout, lighting, and plumbing or electrical moves as the job needs them. We sequence the trades so one does not wreck the last.

04

Protect the house

Floors and adjacent rooms get protected. We clean as we go. A kitchen remodel should not feel like moving out of your own house.

05

Punch list, then done

We walk the finished work together, write the punch list, and finish it before we call the job complete.

Fit and Finish

Cabinets, counters, and the details you live with

Fit and finish matter more than a pretty render. Doors that hang true. Hardware that lines up. Counters that meet the backsplash clean. Lighting that works at 6 a.m. and at dinner.

Typical scope pieces, always project-specific:

  • Cabinets and hardware
  • Counters and backsplash
  • Lighting in the kitchen zone
  • Flooring where the kitchen meets the rest of the house
  • Plumbing and electrical as the plan requires

We do not publish dollar ranges or an average Birmingham kitchen cost. A free estimate is the honest number.

We run the whole kitchen job as the GC. Cabinets, layout, finishes, and the trades the plan needs, under one written scope. That is the work. Not a coordinator hoping the parts show up in the right order.
