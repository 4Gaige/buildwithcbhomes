Meet Joseph & Connor

Co-Owners of CB Built Homes

Joseph Capra grew up in Cahaba Heights and brings nearly a decade of construction management and land development to every job. Connor Barnett is a U.S. Navy veteran with a technical background in nuclear mechanics and nearly a decade of construction management of his own. He is a detail-oriented, do-it-right-the-first-time counterpart.

Together they started CB Built Homes around something they believe has become hard to find: exceptional craftsmanship and genuine attention to detail. They approach every project as if it were their own home, from first concept and design through material selections, construction, and the smallest finishing details.
